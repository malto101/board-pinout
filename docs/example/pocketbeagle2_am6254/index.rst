PocketBeagle 2 — AM6254
========================

This sample uses the PocketBeagle 2 A1 AM6254 configuration. Its focused
SoC table shows TI K3 fixed MUX modes and alternate UART, I2C, SPI, MCAN,
and GPIO functions on the two expansion headers. The board artwork is copied
from Zephyr's PocketBeagle 2 documentation.
